# Attribution

SAANS displays air-quality data from **Open-Meteo.com**, sourced from the
**Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS)** ensemble forecast.

- This is **modeled** data on an approximately **11 km grid**, not a measurement
  from a station at any school.
- Open-Meteo's data is licensed **CC-BY 4.0**; attribution is required and shown
  here and on the dashboard.
- The free Open-Meteo API is for **non-commercial use** (under 10,000 calls/day).

SAANS computes a **PM-only CPCB AQI** from PM2.5 and PM10 concentrations. CPCB's
full index uses three or more pollutants; ours is a documented simplification.
AQI breakpoints follow CPCB's National Air Quality Index (2014).

Every advisory states that it is an estimate and that the reader should use their
own judgment alongside any local reading.
